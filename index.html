Before it hits the rack

Four checks.
No exceptions.

Most used tire lots stack whatever comes off the truck. We turn away about a third of what we're offered.

01

Depth

Gauge reading at the inner, center and outer groove. Uneven wear gets flagged or pulled.

02

Date code

We read the DOT stamp. Nothing older than six years goes on a car, no matter how much tread it has.

03

Inside out

Spread on a lit tire spreader. We look for plugs, cords, cracking and run-flat damage you can't see from outside.

04

Water test

Aired up and dunked. If it bubbles anywhere, it goes to the scrap pile, not the rack.

0

Percent turned away

Out of every hundred tires offered to us last month, thirty one failed one of the four checks. Those never get a price tag.
